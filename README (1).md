# FIELDLINE

## Abstract

FIELDLINE is a proposed offline-first, secure field operations and incident assurance platform intended to support authorised personnel in defence-support, emergency-response, and other connectivity-restricted environments. The project aims to bring secure information handling, incident documentation, evidence integrity, audio diagnostics, and operational-readiness workflows together in a unified local workstation.

The proposed platform combines encrypted data storage and exchange, evidence-package creation and verification, audio waveform and frequency analysis, gesture-assisted user interaction, multilingual documentation support, and configurable standard operating procedure (SOP) checklists. Its offline-first approach is intended to reduce dependence on continuous internet connectivity and external cloud services when recording and managing sensitive field information.

A central component is the Evidence Capsule workflow, which groups incident reports and associated media with metadata and cryptographic hashes so that package integrity can be checked when evidence is exported or imported. The Signal Intelligence workspace is intended to provide practical audio diagnostics, including waveform visualisation, spectrum analysis, spectrograms, and recording-quality measurements. Supporting modules focus on safety and equipment checklists, incident timelines, and structured field reporting.

FIELDLINE is being developed as a modular engineering prototype. Its intended contribution is to explore how privacy-conscious local computing, applied cryptography, signal processing, and accessible interfaces can support reliable field documentation and readiness workflows. Its suitability for any operational defence environment would require further implementation, independent security assessment, hardware testing, and validation against the requirements of the relevant organisation.
